01 Profile
  • New Lineages of Life Group
  • DOE JGI · LBNL
  • Berkeley, California

I lead the New Lineages of Life Group at the DOE Joint Genome Institute, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. We identify new bacterial, archaeal and eukaryotic lineages, and their viruses, in environmental sequence data, using machine learning and multi-omics.

As Biosecurity Lead at DOE JGI, I work on pathogen-agnostic methods to detect and characterize biological threats.
